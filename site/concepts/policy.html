Policy

A rule that maps what the agent knows about the current situation to an action.

01 · the ordinary pressure

Why this idea has to exist

The controller needs a repeatable decision rule, not a separate hand-picked action for every moment.

A common wrong first move is: Store a fixed action sequence. It fails when disturbances and uncertainty mean the state may differ from the planned path.

02 · the object

What the math keeps track of

The working object is a deterministic or stochastic policy. Use it when you need to look for the object that chooses actions during execution.

What the object does: Given a state or observation, output an action or action distribution.

03 · read it with your hands

What to inspect first

Before naming the method, point to a deterministic or stochastic policy in the setup. Ask what a person could measure, command, update, price, or forbid in one small run.

Now apply one command or one update. Given a state or observation, output an action or action distribution. After that operation, ask what changed in the next state, the path, the value, the constraint set, the policy, or the learned model. If nothing concrete changes, the explanation is still only a label.

The world check is the example: A drone policy maps position and velocity errors to thrust commands. Try the same question on another car, drone, rover, robot arm, or reward signal. The concept should still name what changes, what is paid for, and what can fail.

04 · one concrete run

Work it through before naming the formula

A thermostat policy reads temperature and decides heat on or off. A robot policy reads camera, joint, and gripper state and decides a motion command. The policy is the closed-loop rule, not a one-time plan.

the actual math, one level deeper

A policy maps state or observation to action, possibly as a probability distribution. In deployment, the policy's action changes the next input it will see.

05 · boundary

Where the idea stops working

A policy can only react to information available in its input.

When that condition fails, look for this visible break: the policy cannot recover from unseen states or missing observations.

Recognize it in a new problem: Look for the object that chooses actions during execution.
