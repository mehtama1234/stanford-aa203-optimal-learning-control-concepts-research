Policy

A rule that maps what the agent knows about the current situation to an action.

Ordinary Problem

The controller needs a repeatable decision rule, not a separate hand-picked action for every moment.

Naive Approach

Store a fixed action sequence.

Why it fails: Disturbances and uncertainty mean the state may differ from the planned path.

Mathematical Object

A deterministic or stochastic policy.

Operation

Given a state or observation, output an action or action distribution.

Worked Example

A drone policy maps position and velocity errors to thrust commands.

Boundary And Failure

Assumption Boundary

A policy can only react to information available in its input.

Failure Mode

The policy cannot recover from unseen states or missing observations.

Recognize It

Look for the object that chooses actions during execution.
