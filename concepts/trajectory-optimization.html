Trajectory Optimization

Choosing an entire path of states and actions that obeys dynamics while minimizing cost.

01 · the ordinary pressure

Why this idea has to exist

Many control tasks are not about one command but about a full motion plan.

The tempting shortcut is simple: Plan a geometric path first and worry about dynamics later. But that shortcut breaks because a short path may require impossible acceleration, torque, or contact transitions.

02 · the object

What the math keeps track of

The controller keeps track of a state-action trajectory. This answers a practical question: use this term when the solution is a whole planned motion, not just a feedback rule.

The operation is concrete: Optimize the path while enforcing dynamics and constraints.

03 · one concrete run

04 · boundary

Where the idea stops working

The optimized model must represent the important dynamics and constraints.

If that boundary is crossed, the visible failure is this: the trajectory is visually plausible but cannot be executed.
