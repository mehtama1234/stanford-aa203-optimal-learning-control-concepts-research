Drills

Setup drill

Given a drone delivery task, name state, action, dynamics, cost, constraints, and horizon.

Method choice drill

Choose between direct transcription, dynamic programming, MPC, imitation learning, and RL for a warehouse robot.

Bellman drill

Explain why a greedy action fails and write the now-plus-future reasoning in words.

MPC feasibility drill

Find why a short-horizon plan leaves no legal move at the next step.

Reward drill

Diagnose a reward that encourages fast arrival but unsafe braking.

LQR drill

State when a local quadratic approximation is reasonable and when it is unsafe.
